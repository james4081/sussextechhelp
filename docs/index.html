How I Can Help

Practical help with your everyday technology

Whether you are booking for yourself, arranging a visit for a parent, or running a small business from home, I sort things out calmly at your kitchen table or desk.

Slow computer speed-ups

Giving sluggish laptops and desktop PCs a thorough health check and tidy-up so they start up and run smoothly again.

Virus and scam clean-ups

Removing unwanted pop-ups, viruses and suspicious software, and checking your computer and accounts are safe and secure.

New laptop setup and data transfer

Getting your new computer out of the box, moving across your treasured photos, documents and favourites, and setting it up how you like it.

Wi-Fi and printer setup

Sorting out stubborn wireless printers, fixing Wi-Fi dead spots around the house, and connecting your devices reliably.

Phone and tablet help

Friendly help with iPads, iPhones and Android phones — from installing apps and video calling family to freeing up photo storage.

Email and account setup

Untangling password muddles, setting up email across your computer and phone, and helping you sign into everyday online services.

Patient help for beginners and older users

Unhurried, step-by-step guidance at your own pace. We can write down clear notes together so you feel confident after I leave.

Home office and small business IT

Dependable local support for sole traders and home offices — backups, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and keeping your work kit running.

Repairs, Upgrades and Networking

Hardware sorted too

From a tired phone battery to a brand new gaming PC. Any parts are quoted and agreed with you before I order them.

Phone battery replacements

Phone not lasting the day? I replace worn-out batteries in popular iPhone and Android models, with the part price agreed before I start.

Phone and tablet screen replacements

Cracked or unresponsive screen? I fit replacement screens on popular phones and tablets, with a clear quote for the part upfront.

Custom PC builds

Gaming, editing or a quiet home office machine, built to your budget. I help pick the parts, build it, and set it up ready to use.

PC and laptop upgrades

More memory, a fast SSD or a new graphics card can make an older machine feel new, and it often costs far less than replacing it.

Laptop repairs

Worn-out batteries, broken keys and noisy or overheating fans replaced, so a trusty laptop keeps going for years.

Data recovery and transfer

Getting photos and files off a failing or dead computer where it can be done, and moving everything safely onto your new phone or PC.

Home networking and broadband

Router setup and swaps, mesh Wi-Fi, wired points to the office or TV, and pinning down broadband faults so you get the right fix from your provider or Openreach.
